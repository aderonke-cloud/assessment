Egusi Soup


Description: It's a YORUBA traditional meal made with MELON and VEGETABLE

Reciepe Details

Prep time: 15 mins
Cook time: 10 mins
Serves:2
Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients List

  • 2 cups of Egusi (Melon)
  • 10cl bottle of Palm Oil
  • 10 Scotch Bonnet
  • 6 cayenne Pepper
  • 2 medium size bulbs of Onions
  • Locust beans (IRU)

Instruction

  1. Pour your palm oil into a heated pan
  2. Add onions and locust beans
  3. Add your blended scotch bonnet, cayenne pepper and onions
  4. Let it fry a little, then add seasoning cubes and salt to taste
  5. Add your melon mixed with crayfish and meat stock, then cook for 8mins
